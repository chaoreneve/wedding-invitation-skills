# Copywriting guide

- Use the user’s chosen voice or the established invitation tone. Ask about voice only when it is unclear and would materially change the result.
- Preserve names, dates, addresses, room names, and schedule exactly as supplied.
- Keep the cover invitation to one short sentence.
- Keep gallery introductions to about 30–70 Chinese characters.
- Use captions to connect photographs into a progression; avoid describing facts not visible or supplied.
- Keep the closing specific enough to feel personal but free of invented memories.
- Avoid exaggerated promises, clichés stacked together, and language that pressures guests to attend.
- If bilingual copy is requested, treat Chinese as the factual source and verify that both languages contain matching event details.


## Photo lightbox copy

- For supplied gallery photos, provide a short visitor-facing `title` and one concise `caption` by default. A photo integration is not complete with asset labels such as “光影亲吻” or “photo-06” as titles and empty captions. Honor approved wording or an explicit request to omit copy.
- Keep `src` as the asset path and `alt` as a literal, accessible description. These serve different purposes from the expressive title and caption; do not use filenames or sorting labels as finished copy.
- Follow the classic invitation’s short-title-plus-sentence approach. For example: **温柔相依** — “额头轻轻相抵，把这一刻留给彼此。” Or **蓝调烟火** — “夜色渐深，手中的烟火照亮并肩的我们。” Adapt each sentence to the actual image rather than reusing an example indiscriminately.
- Draw on visible gestures, light and mood. Do not invent how the couple met, dates, places, travel or personal memories. Vary captions across the gallery without repeating the same sentiment on every photo.
- Save titles and captions in the source configuration and keep the generated configuration consistent. Check any separately clickable cover and ending photos for intentional copy rather than accidentally empty fields; use the template’s supported metadata shape.
- Verify the rendered lightbox after reloading: click a photo, check that both title and caption belong to that photo, and ensure the complete text is readable on a phone. Check original and looped gallery frames; closing the lightbox should restore the expected gallery behavior. If preview verification fails, report it separately from successful file validation.
